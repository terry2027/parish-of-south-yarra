The Augustinian Tradition

The Order of St Augustine (OSA) has ministered in South Yarra for over 50 years, celebrating community life, spiritual intellectualism, and local service.

We trace our historical roots back to the very foundation of the Melbourne church, following in the rich legacy of Archbishop James Alipius Goold OSA, Melbourne's pioneer bishop.

About our Parish

Welcome to South Yarra Parish

The Parish of South Yarra is a welcoming Catholic community in the inner east of Melbourne, incorporating the congregations of St Joseph's Church and St Thomas Aquinas' Church. We are proudly served by the Order of St Augustine.

Holy Sacraments

Sacraments & Liturgy

The sacramental life of our church is central to our faith. We are delighted to support you on your spiritual journey:

1. Holy Baptisms

Baptisms are celebrated on Sundays. Preparation programs are run regularly to welcome infants and parents. Contact the parish office to book.

2. Weddings & Marriage Preparation

Our beautiful historic churches are beloved wedding venues. Couples must participate in a sacramental marriage preparation program beforehand.

3. Rite of Christian Initiation of Adults (RCIA)

Our RCIA Program concludes annually with adult baptisms and confirmation during the Easter Vigil. If you are exploring joining the Catholic church, we welcome you to join the cohort.

St Joseph Outreach Services (SJOS)

St Joseph Outreach Services (SJOS)

St Joseph Outreach Services (SJOS) is a registered charitable initiative of the South Yarra parish community, dedicated to providing compassionate aid to isolated and disadvantaged individuals.

Key Support Projects

  • Emergency Food Pantry: Providing staple supplies to needy residents in the public housing blocks.
  • Laudato Si Garden: Promoting ecological care, fresh vegetable growing, and urban community participation.
  • Crisis Material Aid: Supplying warm clothing, blankets, and essential household items.

Donations of non-perishable foods and financial contributions are deeply appreciated and are tax-deductible.

Parish Ministries

Active Parish Ministries

Our faith is put into action daily through numerous liturgical, youth, and communal ministries:

1. Liturgical Rosters

Get involved as a Reader, Extraordinary Minister of Communion, Altar Server, or Musician at St Joseph's or St Thomas Aquinas' Churches.

2. IGNITE & Young Adult Ministry (YAM)

A thriving, warm group welcoming students and young professionals for faith studies, social calendar gatherings, and spiritual reflection.

3. Children's Liturgy

Held during the 10:30 AM Sunday Mass at St Joseph's, offering engaging gospel lessons and scriptural activities for elementary school children.
